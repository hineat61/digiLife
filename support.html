Support

Help and information for digiLife Education Edition. Whether you're creating your first experiment or exploring a complex digital ecosystem, resources are available to help you get the most from digiLife.

Getting Started

digiLife includes a Guided Simulator designed to introduce the major parts of an experiment through a structured setup process.

If you're new to digiLife, the Guided Simulator is a good place to begin.

Sandbox Simulator

Sandbox mode provides greater control over populations, environmental conditions, nutrients, mutation, and other experimental parameters.

Use Sandbox when you want to construct your own evolutionary experiments and investigate how changing conditions affect a population.

User Guide

digiLife Education Edition contains an integrated User Guide covering Guided experiments, Sandbox experiments, simulation controls, symbols, and terminology.

Open the Learn section inside digiLife to access these resources.

Simulation Behavior

digiLife models interacting biological systems including inheritance, mutation, metabolism, growth, reproduction, competition, predation, environmental response, aging, and natural selection.

Because populations develop from these interacting systems, different experiments can produce very different outcomes.

Reporting a Problem

If digiLife behaves unexpectedly, please include as much information as possible when requesting support.

Helpful information includes the digiLife version, iPad model, iPadOS version, whether you were using Guided or Sandbox mode, and the steps that led to the problem.

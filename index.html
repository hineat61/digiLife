Learn through experimentation

digiLife Education Edition is an interactive digital-organism simulator designed to make concepts from biology, evolution, ecology, inheritance, and natural selection visible and experimentally accessible.

There is no predetermined evolutionary destination. The populations you observe emerge from inherited variation, environmental conditions, ecological interactions, and the passage of generations.

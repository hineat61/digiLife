Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

Overview

digiLife Education Edition is designed to operate without collecting personal information from its users. Protecting user privacy is part of the design of the application.

Information Collection

digiLife Education Edition does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information or personally identifiable information.

Analytics and Tracking

digiLife Education Edition does not use third-party advertising services or technologies designed to track users across apps or websites.

Simulation Data

Information created while using digiLife, including organisms, populations, experiments, environmental settings, and simulation results, is used to provide the application's simulation functionality.

Simulation information is not transmitted to the developer for advertising, tracking, or profiling.

Children's Privacy

digiLife Education Edition does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Third-Party Services

digiLife Education Edition does not provide personal information to advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties for advertising or tracking purposes.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if digiLife's features or data practices change. Any revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or digiLife Education Edition can be submitted through the digiLife Support page.
